# Data licence

The code in this repository is under the MIT licence (`LICENSE`). The data
this project compiles is under the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International** licence (CC BY 4.0):
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>.

## What the data licence covers

- The roster and candidate CSVs at the repository root.
- The caches under `candidate_profiles_site/data/*.json` and the generated
  files under `candidate_profiles_site/data/` (`profiles.js`, `stategov.js`,
  `federal.js` and the rest), as far as this project's selection,
  arrangement, matching and judgements in them go: which filing belongs to
  which person, who is on which ballot, which link or office is withdrawn,
  and why.
- The prose of the generated pages, including the Know Your Government
  guide.

## How to credit it

"Know Your Candidate (https://github.com/pkplaya626/Know-Your-Candidate),
CC BY 4.0", with a note if you changed the data. Please keep the dates the
data carries (`built`, `asOf`, `fetched`): a figure without its date reads
as current.

## What it does not cover

Material from other sources keeps the terms its source sets, and this
licence grants nothing in it:

- Photographs. The site links portraits where their owners publish them
  (the House Clerk, Congress.gov, Wikimedia Commons, campaign sites, state
  and court sites); none is relicensed here, and each Commons file carries
  its own licence and author.
- Records of the federal government, such as FEC filings, Census files and
  court records, which are in the public domain in the United States.
- Datasets this project reads, such as congress-legislators, Open States,
  Voteview and the market and polling pages. README.md names the source of
  each field; their terms apply to their contents.
