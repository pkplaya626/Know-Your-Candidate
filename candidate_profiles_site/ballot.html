Elections 2026

Your ballot

Every contest on your November 3, 2026 ballot that this site can name, from your ZIP code or your location: the races for Congress and who is in them, the statewide offices and their candidates, whether your seats in the state legislature are up, and the statewide questions in their official words, with the local ones of the most populous counties. Who represents you now is another question: find who represents you.

Sources: the races for Congress and everyone in them as the rest of this site lists them: the candidate field from the Federal Election Commission, primary results from each state's Wikipedia election page, and the November ballot from the certified list of candidates its election office publishes, where this site reads one, each race crediting its own; the statewide offices on the ballot from each state's constitution, statutes and election pages, and their candidates from the election office's own list, each list named and dated where it is shown; the districts a ZIP code or a location falls in from the Census Bureau's boundaries and ZIP Code Tabulation Areas; the key dates from each election office's own pages; the statewide questions from each election office's own list, each with its official text and source; the local questions of the 50 most populous counties (the Census Bureau's 2024 estimates) - each county's own, its countywide districts' and its cities' of 250,000 people or more - from the county election office's, the city clerk's or the state election office's own sample ballot or list, each with its source.

Not listed here: the candidates for the state legislature, local ballot questions elsewhere, and judicial and local contests. Where a state's election office offers a sample ballot, the page links it.
