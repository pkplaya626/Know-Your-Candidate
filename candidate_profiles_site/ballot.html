Elections 2026

Your ballot

Every contest on your November 3, 2026 ballot that this site can name, from your ZIP code or your location: the races for Congress and who is in them, the statewide offices and their candidates, whether your seats in the state legislature are up, and the statewide questions in their official words. Who represents you now is another question: find who represents you.

Sources: the races for Congress and everyone in them as the rest of this site lists them: the candidate field from the Federal Election Commission, primary results from each state's Wikipedia election page, and the November ballot from the certified list of candidates its election office publishes, where this site reads one, each race crediting its own; the statewide offices on the ballot from each state's constitution, statutes and election pages, and their candidates from the election office's own list, each list named and dated where it is shown; the districts a ZIP code or a location falls in from the Census Bureau's boundaries and ZIP Code Tabulation Areas; the key dates from each election office's own pages; the statewide questions from each election office's own list, each with its official text and source.

Not listed here: the candidates for the state legislature, local ballot questions, and judicial and local contests. Where a state's election office links a sample ballot, the page links it.
