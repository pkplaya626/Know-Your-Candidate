  1. CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT - REQUIRE PHOTO ID FOR VOTING: Require Photo ID for All Voters Proposed Constitutional Amendment (source)
  2. CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT - MAXIMUM INCOME TAX RATE OF 3.5%: Lower Taxes for All NC Proposed Constitutional Amendment (source)
  3. CONSTITUTIONAL AMENDMENT - PROPERTY TAX LEVY LIMIT: Property Tax Levy Limit Proposed Constitutional Amendment (source)

Each is linked to the page its official text is on. This site does not say what a yes or a no vote does.

Local questions - a county's, a city's or a school district's - are not listed here.
