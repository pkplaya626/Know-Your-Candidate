Statewide questions on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot

3 statewide questions are on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot, as the Georgia Secretary of State lists them: Proposed Constitutional Amendments, General Election November 3, 2026: Constitutional Amendments 1-3 and Summaries of Amendments (Georgia Secretary of State), read Oct. 19, 2026.

  1. Amendment 1: Increases the maximum qualifying acreage for assessment and taxation as bona fide conservation use property. (source)
  2. Amendment 2: Provides for the nonpartisan election of all probate court judges. (source)
  3. Amendment 3: Authorizes creation of Georgia Next Generation 9-1-1 Fund for 9-1-1 systems expansion, maintenance, and operation. (source)

Each is linked to the page its official text is on. This site does not say what a yes or a no vote does.

Local questions - a county's, a city's or a school district's - are not listed here.
