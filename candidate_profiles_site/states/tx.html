Statewide questions on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot

No statewide question is on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot.

This site read General Election Sample Ballot, November 3, 2026 (Texas Secretary of State) on Oct. 19, 2026, and it prints no statewide question. A statewide question would be printed on every ballot.

Local questions - a county's, a city's or a school district's - are not listed here.
