Statewide questions that were on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot

4 statewide questions were on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot, as the South Dakota Secretary of State lists them: Potential Ballot Questions: 2026 General Election Ballot Measures (South Dakota Secretary of State), read Oct. 19, 2026.

  1. Constitutional Amendment I: An Amendment to the South Dakota Constitution that Repeals Expanded Medicaid Coverage if Federal Funding for the Program Drops below 90%. (source)
  2. Constitutional Amendment J: An Amendment to the South Dakota Constitution Clarifying that an Individual Must Be a Citizen of the United States to be Eligible to Vote. (source)
  3. Constitutional Amendment K: An Amendment to the South Dakota Constitution Establishing the Trust for Unclaimed Property Fund. (source)
  4. Constitutional Amendment L: An Amendment to the South Dakota Constitution Requiring that a Proposed Constitutional Amendment or Revision Receive an Affirmative Vote of Sixty Percent of the Votes Cast. (source)

Each is linked to the page its official text is on. This site does not say what a yes or a no vote does.
