Sources: membership, contact details and committee rosters from the congress-legislators dataset; the candidate field and campaign finance from the Federal Election Commission; primary results from each state's Wikipedia election page, as last read on ; the November ballot, where this site reads one, from the certified list of candidates its election office publishes, credited on each race it sets; portraits from the House of Representatives, Congress.gov, Wikimedia Commons and candidates' campaign websites, each credited under the photograph on the profile, a Wikimedia Commons file with its author and licence; the statewide questions from the election office's own list, voter guide or official sample ballots, each with its official text and source, read by hand.

Who counts as a candidate: everyone the FEC records as having raised or spent more than $5,000 for the 2026 cycle (52 U.S.C. §30101(2)). People no longer in a race - beaten in a primary, withdrawn, or left off a certified list - stay on record and are shown under each race.
