Statewide questions on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot

4 statewide questions are on the Nov 3, 2026 ballot, as the Arkansas Secretary of State lists them: Initiatives and Referenda - Arkansas Secretary of State, read Oct. 19, 2026.

  1. Issue No. 1: A Constitutional Amendment to be Known as "The Citizens Only Voting Amendment" and Providing That Only a Citizen of the United States Meeting the Qualifications of an Elector Under the Arkansas Constitution May Vote in an Election in this State. (source)
  2. Issue No. 2: A Constitutional Amendment to Amend Arkansas Constitution, Article 2, § 5, to Protect the Right to Keep and Bear Arms. (source)
  3. Issue No. 3: A Constitutional Amendment Concerning Economic Development in the State of Arkansas; and Authorizing the General Assembly to Provide for the Creation of Economic Development Districts Within Cities, Counties, or Cooperative Areas to Promote Economic Development Within the Economic Development District. (source)
  4. Issue No. 4: Arkansas Water, Waste Disposal, and Pollution Abatement Facilities Financing Act of 2025. (source)

Each is linked to the page its official text is on. This site does not say what a yes or a no vote does.

Local questions - a county's, a city's or a school district's - are not listed here.
