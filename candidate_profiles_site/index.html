Fields shown as Not disclosed have no public filing behind them, and No data means no source has been recorded, including where a roster held only a generic placeholder. Neither is counted as a finding, and neither is searchable.

Who counts as a candidate: everyone the FEC records as having raised or spent more than $5,000 for the 2026 cycle — the point at which federal law treats a person as a candidate (52 U.S.C. §30101(2)). Filings below that are still counted per race, so a seat is never described as uncontested when somebody has filed for it.
